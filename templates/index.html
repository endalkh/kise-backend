What Kise does

Two kinds of spending. Two calendars. One clear picture.

Most trackers treat rent and coffee as the same thing, and the Ethiopian calendar as a display setting. Kise does neither.

Two calendars, one truth

Enter a date as ሐምሌ ፲፭ or as 22 July. Kise stores one and shows both, exactly, across leap years and century boundaries. A month is an Ethiopian month if you want it to be.

Fixed apart from everyday

Rent, internet, school fees are commitments. Taxi and coffee are choices. Kise keeps them on separate lines so the total finally means something.

Recurring, without drift

A commitment anchored to ሐምሌ produces exactly one occurrence per Ethiopian month, forever. Viewed in a Gregorian month it still lands where you expect.

Works offline

The calendar engine runs on your phone. Labels, month boundaries and ጳጉሜን are exact with no signal, and identical to what the server computes.

Amharic or English

The whole interface, not just the month names. Switch with one tap; your data does not change, only its language.

እንደምን አሉ!

Your currency, your colours

One currency per person, ETB by default, counted in whole santim so nothing drifts by a cent. Four palettes — forest, sunset, burgundy, ocean — around the same calm neutrals.

The calendar

Thirteen months, and the thirteenth is not a footnote.

Twelve months of thirty days, then ጳጉሜን — five days, six in a leap year. Kise stores every date in Gregorian and derives the Ethiopian view at the edges, so the arithmetic is exact and the labels are always right.

New year on 11 September
Or the 12th, in the year before a Gregorian leap year. Kise knows which.
One September, three months
September 2026 spans ነሐሴ, ጳጉሜን and መስከረም. Kise labels the whole span.

How it works

Set it up once. Then just spend.

  1. 01

    Pick your calendar and language

    Ethiopian or Gregorian, Amharic or English, ETB or another currency. Change any of them later; your history follows.

  2. 02

    Add your commitments

    Rent on ሐምሌ 1, internet on the 15th. Each one recurs by itself, and you tick it off when it is paid.

  3. 03

    Log the day as it happens

    Taxi, lunch, airtime. Two taps each. The dashboard shows the month in both calendars, against your budget.
